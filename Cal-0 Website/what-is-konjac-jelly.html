Konjac is the ingredient behind the name.

Konjac flour comes from the konjac plant’s underground corm, often described as a root or tuber. Its main component is glucomannan. In food, konjac flour can help thicken mixtures and form a gel, which is why it appears in jelly-style products.

For CAL-0, mango is the flavor and konjac powder is one ingredient that contributes to the texture. Our mango konjac jelly comes in an individually packaged, squeezable pouch.

What’s in a CAL-0 mango pouch?

The current ingredients are water, mango juice, erythritol, konjac powder, carrageenan, ascorbic acid (Vitamin C), citric acid, potassium chloride and potassium sorbate (preservative).

The complete recipe matters when you’re choosing a snack. The word “konjac” identifies an ingredient; the Nutrition Facts panel gives you the nutrient amounts for the finished pouch.

The numbers, per pouch.

One 150 mL CAL-0 mango pouch contains 10 calories, 300 mg Vitamin C and 0 g total fat. It contains 3 g total sugars, including 3 g added sugars.
