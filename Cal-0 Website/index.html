Meet the pouch

A lighter snack.
A refreshing squeeze.

Konjac gives Cal-0 its jelly-style texture. Enjoy it chilled, a little at a time.

  1. Chill it. Pop it in the fridge or cooler.
  2. Squeeze it. Gently squeeze the closed pouch to mix the jelly.
  3. Enjoy it. Open, squeeze a little at a time and chew thoroughly.
