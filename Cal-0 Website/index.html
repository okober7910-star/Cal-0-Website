Three simple steps

How to Enjoy

  1. 1 Chill It
    Pop it in the fridge or cooler for a refreshing experience.
  2. 2 Squeeze It
    Give the pouch a gentle squeeze before opening to mix the jelly.
  3. 3 Enjoy
    Squeeze a little at a time and chew thoroughly.

What is konjac jelly?+

Konjac jelly is a jelly-style snack made with konjac, a plant-based ingredient known for its unique texture.

How do I eat it?+

Chill it, gently squeeze the pouch to break up the jelly, then enjoy a little at a time and chew thoroughly.
