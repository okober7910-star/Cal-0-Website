How Cal-0 handles information submitted through this website.

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cal-0 LLC (“Cal-0,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website, contact us, sign up for product updates, or place an online order.

Information we may collect

We may receive your name, email address, phone number, message, retailer or business information, shipping details, and other information you choose to submit.

When you place an order, payment information is collected and processed by the payment provider linked from this website. Cal-0 should not receive your full payment-card number from a hosted payment page.

This website uses browser local storage to remember cart quantity on your device. The website package does not include advertising pixels or third-party analytics by default. Information may be shared with service providers that support website hosting, forms, email, payment processing, shipping, professional advice, security, or legal compliance. We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including business, tax, warranty, dispute, and legal requirements. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the instructions in the email or by contacting us. Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how personal information is handled.

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will be changed when a revised policy is published.
