Privacy Policy

How Cal-0 handles information submitted through this website.

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cal-0 LLC (“Cal-0,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website, contact us, sign up for product updates, or place an online order.

Information we may collect

Information you provide

We may receive your name, email address, phone number, message, retailer or business information, shipping details, and other information you choose to submit.

Orders and payment information

When you place an order, payment information is collected and processed by the payment provider linked from this website. Cal-0 should not receive your full payment-card number from a hosted payment page.

Website and device information

The current website does not store cart quantities or include advertising tracking or third-party analytics in its site code. The contact form prepares a draft in your email app; it does not automatically send or store a submission. Hosting and image providers may create standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and timestamps. Third-party checkout and social-media services have their own privacy practices. If Cal-0 adds analytics, advertising tools, or additional cookies, this policy should be updated before those tools are used.

How we may use information

  • To respond to questions and retailer or wholesale inquiries.
  • To send product, store, or flavor updates when you ask to receive them.
  • To process and support online orders.
  • To protect the website, investigate misuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve Cal-0 products, marketing, and customer service.

How information may be shared

Information may be shared with service providers that support website hosting, forms, email, payment processing, shipping, professional advice, security, or legal compliance. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a merger, financing, sale, or other business transaction. Cal-0 does not use this website to sell personal information for money.

Data retention and security

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including business, tax, warranty, dispute, and legal requirements. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the instructions in the email or by contacting us. Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how personal information is handled.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will be changed when a revised policy is published.

Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to hello@cal-0.com.
