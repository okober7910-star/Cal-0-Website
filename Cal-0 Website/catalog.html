Ingredients

How to enjoy it

Best served chilled. Before opening, gently squeeze the closed pouch to mix the jelly. Open it, enjoy a little at a time and chew thoroughly.

Not for children under 3. Follow the directions and warnings printed on the pouch.

The unopened product is shelf-stable. Keep it cool and dry. Refrigerate after opening and consume within 24 hours, following the directions on your package.
